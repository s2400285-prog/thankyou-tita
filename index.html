A Letter for you Tita

Dear Tita, 🤍

I just want to take a little time to thank you sincerely, Tita. Thank you for your beautiful message and for the kindness you have shown me. Honestly, bisan bag o pa lang kita nagkilalahay, I already feel grateful nga nakilala ko kamo. Your words touched my heart, and I truly appreciate how you welcomed me and considered me as part of your family.

Most of all, Tita, thank you for Julie. Thank you for raising a daughter who has brought so much happiness into my life. I appreciate her more than words can explain, and I am thankful for every moment I get to spend with her. The kindness she shows, the efforts she makes, and the love she shares mean so much to me. I know that behind the wonderful person she is today is a mother who has loved, guided, and cared for her. For that, I will always be grateful to you.

I also want to thank you for your encouraging words about being part of your family. It means a lot to me, Tita. I believe that blessings are not only seen through finances or material things. Sometimes, God blesses us through the people He brings into our lives, through the kindness we receive, and through the relationships that help us grow. I feel blessed that God allowed me to meet Julie and get to know your family.

I know that life will not always be easy. There may be days when things go well and days when we face challenges, misunderstandings, or struggles. But I pray that God will always guide us, give us wisdom, and teach us to remain patient, humble, and faithful. As you said, He is the source of everything, and I believe that keeping Him at the center of our lives will help us face whatever comes our way.

Tita, I want you to know that I will do my best to take care of Julie, respect her, support her dreams, listen to her, and treat her with the love and sincerity she deserves. I know I still have a lot to learn and many ways to grow, but I want my actions to show that my intentions toward her are genuine. I will also remember what you said about taking care of myself, because I know that becoming a better person is part of learning how to love others well.

I pray that God blesses you and your entire family with good health, peace, happiness, and abundant grace. May He always protect your home, strengthen your faith, provide for your needs, and guide you through every season of life. I hope that we will continue building a good relationship filled with respect, understanding, and love.

Thank you again, Tita, for your kindness, your acceptance, and your heartfelt prayers. I may not always know the perfect words to express my gratitude, but please know that I sincerely appreciate you. I am happy that I got to know you, and I hope to create more beautiful memories with Julie and your family in the days to come.

May God continue to bless you, Tita, and keep our families under His loving protection. I will always be thankful for the beautiful people He has placed in my life.

With respect, love, and a grateful heart,
James 🤍
